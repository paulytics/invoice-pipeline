AI Invoice & Receipt Pipeline

Drop an invoice or a phone photo of a receipt into a Google Drive folder. It is read by AI, checked like an accountant would check it, and filed. People only see the documents that need them.

How it works

DetectNew file in the Drive inbox
ExtractGemini returns structured JSON
ValidateMaths, dates, confidence, 0–100 score
DeduplicateSupplier + invoice no. + total
RouteSheet, folder and Telegram alert

What makes it reliable

  • Doesn't trust the AI blindlyEvery extraction is checked against accounting rules before it is accepted.
  • Human in the loopReviewers fix a row in Google Sheets and type APPROVE. The fix is re-validated and posted.
  • Secure by designPrompt injection, spreadsheet formula injection and HTML injection are all handled.
  • Fails safelyRetries, error alerts with a link to the failed run, and nothing silently lost.
  • Daily summaryVolume, auto-approval rate, duplicates caught and time saved, sent to Telegram.
  • Tested25 automated tests, including an end-to-end simulation of the workflows.
