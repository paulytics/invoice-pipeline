Privacy Policy

AI Invoice Pipeline · Last updated 15 November 2026

The AI Invoice Pipeline ("the app") is a personal automation built with n8n. It processes invoices and receipts that its owner places in their own Google Drive and records the results in their own Google Sheet. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data the app accesses

With the permission you grant through Google sign-in, the app accesses:

  • Google Drive: files placed in the designated Inbox folder, which it reads and moves to other designated folders.
  • Google Sheets: the designated spreadsheet, which it reads and adds rows to.

The app does not access any other files, emails, contacts or Google account data.

How the data is used

Documents are sent to Google's Gemini API to extract invoice details (such as supplier, date and amounts). The extracted details are written to the designated spreadsheet, and short notifications are sent to the owner's Telegram account. The data is used only to provide this processing.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone other than the services needed to run the app: Google (Drive, Sheets and Gemini) and Telegram (notifications). The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

The app runs on infrastructure controlled by its owner. Access credentials are stored encrypted in n8n. Documents and extracted data remain in the owner's Google Drive and Google Sheets.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
